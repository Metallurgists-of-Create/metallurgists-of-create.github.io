# Our Stance on AI

The Metallurgists of Create are strongly against the usage of Generative AI in creative spaces and will never use it to develop any part of our projects, be that code, text or assets. 
We consider our projects works of art and have precautions in place to prevent people utilising our work to train AI models.

## Our Licence
All of our projects are licenced under [MIT NON-AI]((https://metallurgists-of-create.github.io/about-us/licence)), a licence that we will not hesitate to enforce.
It forbids any of our repositories to be used in training any AI or LLM and forbids any modification of our work to be assisted via the use of AI.

If you do decide to fork our work and are found using AI to generate code in that fork we will not hesitate to issue a DMCA to take it down.
Any violation of our licence will also lead to you being blocked from accessing our repositories.

## Contributing
Contributions to our projects are always welcome, be they issues or pull requests, so long as they are respectful, adhere to the licence, and follow the contributing guidelines found in the repository.
### No AI/LLM Policy
We will not accept contributions made with the assistance of generative AI *no matter the form or extent*.
<br><br>
Pull requests which violate this policy
- Will be assumed to be acting in malice
- Will be rejected with prejudice

Additionally, the 'author' may be blocked from accessing our repositories without being informed.
